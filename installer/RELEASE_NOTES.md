## Download for Windows

Download **`LeonidaIntelligence-Setup-….exe`** from *Assets* below, then double-click it.

- **Windows 10 (1809+) or 11, 64-bit.** No admin rights needed. Windows asks for permission once,
  to let your phone and tablet connect.
- **Includes everything it needs.** Node.js and Git come bundled.
- **Downloads the map on first install** (about 130 MB) from the GTADB community project.
- **The installer isn't code-signed yet.** If Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click
  **More info → Run anyway**. The `.sha256` file lets you check the download.

After installing, Leonida lives in the notification area next to the clock. Click it to open the
map. Right-click → **Open on my phone or tablet** shows the address for your other devices.

Linux, Docker and running from source: see the
[installation guide](https://github.com/thethriftypilot/leonida-intelligence/blob/main/docs/installation.md).
